PC Assemble

During last week’s PC assemble lab, I able to improve my familiarity with hardware components such as RAM, hard disks, CPU and CPU cooler. Since the motherboard was already installed in the casing, our primary task was to install and connect the remaining internal components.

We learned to dissembled and also assembled the PC and here are showing assembly process:

  1. Inserted the CPU into the CPU socket
  2. Secured the CPU fan over the processor by using screwdriver.
  3. Installed the hard disk drive and screw it securely.
  4. Inserted the RAM sticks into the DIMM slots until they clicked into place.
  5. Positioned and screwed the Power Supply Unit (PSU) into the casing.
  6. Connected all the internal cables including power connectors and data buses.
  7. Closed the outer casing panels.
